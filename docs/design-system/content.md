# Flocka Content Model

## Content voice

Short, direct and tactile. Avoid agency jargon. Talk about ideas, skin, marks, process and permanence.

## Demo copy

### Hero

- Eyebrow: `CUSTOM TATTOO STUDIO / BERLIN`
- Headline: `FLOCKA`
- Supporting line: `Tattoo work with old-school soul, sharp composition and a point of view.`
- Primary action: `BOOK A SESSION`
- Secondary action: `VIEW SELECTED WORK`

### Work

- `BLACKWORK RAVEN` — `High contrast / custom piece`
- `RED FLASH STUDY` — `Flash / available design`
- `SACRED GEOMETRY` — `Ornamental / healed work`

### Services

- `BLACKWORK` — Strong silhouettes, deep blacks and graphic structure.
- `ORNAMENTAL` — Pattern, balance and details that hold up over time.
- `FINE LINE` — Quiet marks with a sharp, deliberate edge.
- `FLASH` — Classic symbols, redrawn for the person wearing them.

### Process

- `IDEA` — Bring the story, reference or loose thought.
- `SKETCH` — Build the composition around your body and your intention.
- `SESSION` — Take the time to place the work properly.
- `HEALED` — Care for it, live with it, come back for the next one.

### Contact

- Heading: `LET'S MAKE IT PERMANENT`
- Supporting line: `Tell me what you want to carry with you.`
- Action: `START A BOOKING`
