# Flocka Tattoo

Flocka Tattoo is the official website of Sascha Schlüter, a tattoo artist based in Köln, Germany. The practice focuses on individual tattoos, Blackwork, Fine Line, Schwarz-Rot, selected colour work, Ornamentik, Flash tattoos and cover-up consultations.
